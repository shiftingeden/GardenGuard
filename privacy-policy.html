🌻 Garden Guard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Garden Guard is a single-player tower-defence game for iPhone and iPad. We designed it to respect your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Garden Guard does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no logins, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. The game works entirely on your device, including offline.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not gather usage analytics or device identifiers.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — current level, coins, unlocked tools, best wave and audio settings — is saved locally using Apple's standard on-device storage. This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed if you delete the app.

In-app purchase

Garden Guard offers a single optional one-time purchase (Full Garden) that unlocks all levels and features. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Garden Guard is family-friendly and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no data at all, there is nothing for a parent or guardian to request, correct or delete from our side.

Bug reports

If you choose to use the in-app Report a bug option, it simply opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent unless you personally choose to send the email, and any information you include is shared at your discretion.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
