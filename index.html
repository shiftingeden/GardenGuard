Frequently asked

How do I unlock all the levels and tools?
The free version includes the first 8 maps. A one-time "Full Garden" purchase unlocks all 30 maps, every tool, the Nuclear Option, 2× fast-forward, and all save slots.

I bought Full Garden but it's locked on a new device.
Open the in-app shop or paywall and tap Restore Purchase — your unlock is tied to your Apple ID and restores for free.

Does the game work offline?
Yes — Bug Off! is fully playable without an internet connection.
