Garden Guard is a single-player tower-defence game for iPhone and iPad. Plant your defences, hold the line against waves of pests, and keep the garden standing.

I paid for Full Garden and it's still locked

Open the shop and tap Restore purchases, while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. The unlock follows your Apple Account rather than the device, so a new phone restores rather than re-buys.

The purchase screen won't load

That usually means the App Store couldn't be reached. Check your connection and try again in a moment. If you were charged and nothing unlocked, email me with the date and I'll sort it out.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on the device itself. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears it, and it does not transfer to a new phone. Your purchase does transfer — restore it as above.

A level feels impossible

Most walls come down to placement rather than upgrades: cheap towers early to bank coins, then a few strong ones on the corners where enemies slow down. If a particular wave has you stuck, email me which level and I'll tell you the line that works.

The music or sound effects won't play

Check the in-game audio settings first, then that your phone isn't on silent. The game respects the ringer switch.

Does the game work offline?

Yes, entirely. Garden Guard needs no connection to play — only the App Store purchase itself does.

Privacy
